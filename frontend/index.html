Momentum alignment

The selected CE/PE premium and the index trend should agree; 5-minute EMA, RSI and VWAP help describe momentum.

Liquidity filters

Market depth, open interest, bid/ask spread and relative volume qualify the setup. Displayed orders can change instantly.

Risk gates

No BUY setup on missing or stale data, wide spreads, weak liquidity or outside normal trading hours. Exchange holidays are not yet verified.

Evidence first

Validate with walk-forward testing, fees, slippage, drawdown and paper trades before considering live order automation.
